## Heat, hunters, civilians, combat, death and finales

### 0. Conventions used in this section
- **Units.** Unreal uses cm, and every number here has been converted to **metres / seconds / m/s**. Config names keep their cm values in brackets, e.g. `SoldierSpawnMin` (10000 cm). "2D distance" means horizontal distance only (x/z on the ground plane in Three.js). "3D distance" measures between character centres.
- **Authority.** All rules in this section run only on the **host**. That covers heat, populations, AI, damage, finales and all randomness. Clients receive replicated state and broadcast messages.
- **Director tick.** The heat/population "director" runs every **0.25 s**, in this order:
  1. Prune destroyed bots.
  2. Remove corpses whose timer has run out.
  3. Recount `passportsMissing`.
  4. If `phase == Escaped`: remove every soldier, run the civilian update, stop.
  5. Otherwise: shot detection → heat update → finale update → soldier update → civilian update.

  The **hunter tick** (helicopter, tank and roadblock spawn/removal) runs every **2 s**.
- **Who is who.**
  - **Human/fugitive** = a player-controlled character.
  - **Soldier** = hostile AI.
  - **Civilian** = walker AI.
  - **Living** = health > 0 and not dying.
- **Character body** (Lyra values, keep them on the web): capsule radius 0.40 m, half-height 0.90 m. The character "location" is the capsule centre, 0.90 m above the ground. The **eye** is location + 0.80 m, i.e. 1.70 m above the ground.
- **Teams.** Fugitives are team 1. Soldiers **and civilians** are both team 2. Civilians are told apart by their character type, not their team.
  - Gunfire damage applies only between different teams. Fugitives can hurt soldiers and civilians, but not each other (no friendly fire). Soldiers cannot hurt soldiers or civilians.
  - Soldier AI targets only team 1. **Soldiers never target civilians.**
  - Tank shells and tank crushing ignore teams and kill anyone.
  - Note: `Docs/escape_design.md` says civilians are on "no team", but the code puts them on team 2. Follow the code.
- **Line of sight (LOS)** for every director check means: a ray against **static world geometry only** (buildings, walls, barriers, props). It **ignores all characters, all vehicles, and anything attached to them** (seated drivers).
  - Exceptions: the helicopter spotlight and tank traces ignore only themselves and their target, so cars and other props block them.
  - Web: give static geometry its own Rapier collision group and cast rays with a filter for that group only (or use a BVH over the building meshes). For the helicopter and tank, also include vehicle bodies.
- **Money format:** `₪` followed by the number with thousands separators, e.g. `₪12,345`. Negative values are written `-₪12,345`.

### 1. Heat and stars

**State on the host (replicate it to all clients):**
- `heat`: float, 0..500.
- `searchAlpha`: 0..1.
- `lastHeatGain` and `lastSeen`: timestamps, both starting at −1000.
- `unseenTime`: float.

**Stars** = `clamp(ceil(heat / 100), 0, 5)`. So heat 0 is 0 stars, 0.01–100 is 1 star, 100.01–200 is 2 stars, …, 400.01–500 is 5 stars. Heat is **not** rounded to whole stars: 112 is 2 stars, and one decay step takes it to 12 (1 star).

**`addHeat(amount, reason)`:**
- Ignored when `phase == Escaped`.
- Otherwise `heat = clamp(heat + max(amount, 0), 0, 500)`, `lastHeatGain = now`, `unseenTime = 0`, `searchAlpha = 0`.
- If the star count went up, broadcast `Wanted level N`.
- Even `addHeat(0)` resets the grace timers.

**`setHeatAtLeast(x)`** = `addHeat(max(0, x − heat))`. It always resets the grace period, even when heat is already ≥ x.

**`lowerHeat(x)`** (Lay low): `heat = max(0, heat − x)`, `unseenTime = 0`, `searchAlpha = 0`. It does **not** touch `lastHeatGain` and does not broadcast (the shop sends its own line).

#### 1.1 Crimes (heat gains)

| Event | Heat | Exact rule |
|---|---|---|
| Gunshot by a human | **+12** (`ShotHeat`) | Each director tick, per shooter: if the shooter's equipped ranged weapon has fired since the last tick **and** (a living civilian is within **50 m 2D** of the shooter **or** a living soldier is within **80 m 2D**) → one +12. At most once per 0.25 s tick per shooter, so continuous fire is about +48 heat/s. Distances are measured from the shooter's position at tick time, not from the impact. Soldiers' shots never add heat. |
| Civilian killed by a human (gun) | **+100** (`CivilianKillHeat`) | Broadcast `A civilian was killed. That never pays.` |
| …and witnessed | **+100 more** (`WitnessedKillHeat`), total +200 in one `addHeat` | Witnessed = any living soldier within **100 m 3D** of the victim's location with LOS from the soldier's eye to the victim location + 0.5 m. Broadcast `A soldier saw you kill a civilian!` instead of the line above. |
| Soldier killed by a human (gun) | **+40** (`SoldierKillHeat`) | No broadcast. |
| Hold-up (robbery) | **+40**, **+60 more** if witnessed (total +100) | Witnessed = a living soldier within 100 m 3D of the **robber**, with LOS to robber location + 0.5 m. See §7.6. |
| Civilian run over by a human-driven car (≥ 8 m/s) | **+100**, +100 more if witnessed | Witness test on the victim's location. Broadcast: none. |
| Soldier run over by a human-driven car | **+40** | |
| Bank heist started ("Rob the vault (alarm!)") | heat **at least 300** | `setHeatAtLeast(300)`. This broadcasts `Wanted level 3` if stars rose, plus the heist line `<Name> is robbing <Bank>! Stay within 25 m of the vault for 40 s`. |
| Boat or border crossing paid | heat **forced to 500** | `addHeat(500 − heat)`. Broadcasts `Wanted level 5` if stars rose. The airport does **not** change heat. |

**What causes no heat:**
- Kills by soldiers, tanks, AI traffic, or falls.
- Any kill whose instigator is the victim itself (self-destruct damage).
- Kills of humans.

**Kills never give money.** Bots drop nothing. The only cash dropped on death is the victim's own carried bag.

#### 1.2 Being seen
Every director tick, `lastSeen = now` if **any living soldier** is within **100 m 3D** of **any living human** with LOS from the soldier's eye to the human's eye. There is no view cone (it is omnidirectional).

The helicopter spotlight also sets `lastSeen = now` (`noteSeen()`, §4).

`soldierSees(location, range = 100 m)` is the witness test used in §1.1: any living soldier within range (3D) with LOS from its eye to location + 0.5 m.

#### 1.3 Grace, search and decay
Every director tick, with dt = 0.25 s:
- `searching = heat > 0 && phase == Playing && now − lastHeatGain ≥ 8 s && now − lastSeen ≥ 8 s` (`HeatGraceSeconds`).
- If not searching: `unseenTime = 0`, `searchAlpha = 0`, done.
- Otherwise `unseenTime += 0.25`. When `unseenTime ≥ 15 s` (`StarDecaySeconds`):
  - `unseenTime = 0` and `heat = max(0, heat − 100)`.
  - Broadcast `They lost your trail: wanted level N` (N = new stars) if heat > 0, else `The heat is off`.
- `searchAlpha = clamp(unseenTime / 15, 0, 1)`.
- After a decay step the next step starts at once. There is no new 8 s grace unless heat is gained or someone is seen.
- There is **no decay during a finale.**

**HUD bar** (shown only when stars > 0, under the five stars): dark track.
- If `searchAlpha > 0`: a yellow fill (rgba 1, 0.8, 0.25, 0.75) of width = searchAlpha, labelled **`SEARCHING`**.
- Otherwise: a full red bar pulsing with alpha `0.35 + 0.35·(0.5 + 0.5·sin(6·t))`, labelled **`WANTED`**.

#### 1.4 Other heat resets
- **Team wipe:** heat 0, `unseenTime` 0, `searchAlpha` 0 (§11).
- **Escape completed:** heat 0 (§12).
- **Lay low** at the shop: offered only while stars > 0, costs ₪2,000, gives −100 heat, and broadcasts `<Name> paid to lay low: -1 star`.

### 2. Soldiers: population

**Soldiers per star** (`SoldiersPerStar`, not set in the ini, so the code default applies):

| Stars | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Target per area | 0 | 2 | 6 | 10 | 16 | 24 |

- **Hard cap:** `MaxSoldiers` = **24** in total.
- **During a boat or crossing finale** the per-area target is **24** whatever the heat (the "army"). An airport finale follows the stars (normally 0).

**Areas.** Living humans are grouped by the city inset they stand in. All humans out on the highways (outside every inset) form **one** shared "highway" area.
- Each living soldier counts toward the area of its nearest human (2D), but only if that human is within **350 m** (`SoldierDespawnDistance`). Strays further away count for no area.
- Wanted = `min(perArea × numberOfAreas, 24)`.
- Players in two cities each get the full count, up to the cap.

**Per director tick** (only when at least one living human exists; otherwise nothing spawns or despawns):
1. **Strays.** Each living soldier remembers the last time it was within 350 m 2D of any living human. Once it has been farther than that for **30 s** continuously, remove it. That tick it is not counted.
2. `keep = min(wanted + (anyRoadblockExists ? 6 : 0), 24)`. Roadblock guards come on top of the star count.
3. If `living > keep`: remove the **one** living soldier farthest from its nearest human, then stop for this tick.
4. Else, if `living < wanted`, at least **0.5 s** has passed since the last soldier spawn, and a needy area exists, spawn **one** soldier anchored on a random human of the needy area. The needy area is the one with `bots < perArea` and the fewest bots.

**Spawn point (ring spawn):**
- Candidates are street walk points (road centre-line samples, highways every 60 m; see the World section) at **100–180 m 2D** from the anchor (`SoldierSpawnMin` 10000 / `SoldierSpawnMax` 18000 cm).
- If the ring is empty (a sparse highway), use the single nearest walk point beyond 100 m.
- Make up to **40** random picks. Accept the first that **no human can see**: LOS from every human's eye to point + 1.5 m is blocked.
- If none is hidden, use the farthest pick tried (including the initial random one).
- Spawn 1 m above the ground point, facing a random direction.
- If the anchor died, use a random living human.

**Corpses:** dead soldiers are deleted **6 s** after they were first seen dead. There is no respawn; the director simply spawns new soldiers.

**After the escape:** all soldiers are deleted every tick, and nothing more spawns.

**Display name:** `Soldier`.

**Variants:** assigned round-robin in spawn order, cycling through the four looks. All variants carry the **rifle** and share the same AI. They differ only in model and armour:

| # | Variant | Armour | Max HP (100 / (1 − armour/2)) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | rifleman | 0.3 | 117.6 |
| 1 | officer | 0.0 | 100 |
| 2 | heavy | 0.8 | 166.7 |
| 3 | marksman | 0.2 | 111.1 |

Web: soldiers are plain AI agents on the host. Send position, yaw, animation state and health in the 20 Hz snapshots (spec 05 H16) and interpolate.

### 3. Soldier AI (web replacement for Lyra's shooter behaviour tree)

The Lyra bot's numbers live only in binary assets. The values marked "(Lyra)" were read from `Plugins/GameFeatures/ShooterCore/Content/Bot/*.uasset` property data. Everything marked (choose) is a stated default.

**Perception and targeting**
- Sight radius **50 m**; lose sight at **60 m**; memory of a lost target **1 s** (Lyra).
- View cone **180°** (±90°; Lyra leaves the engine default).
- Sight also needs LOS from eye to target eye against static geometry, re-checked every **0.2 s** (choose).
- Being damaged reveals the attacker at once (Lyra damage sense).
- Target = **nearest living fugitive anywhere on the map** (Lyra's find-target query scores all enemies by inverse distance). A perceived enemy is preferred over an unperceived nearer one (choose). Re-pick the target every 1 s (choose).
- Never target civilians or soldiers.

**States**
1. **Hunt** (target not perceived): path toward the target's current position along the street/walk-point graph at run speed **6 m/s** (Lyra default walk/run). Re-path every 1 s (choose).
2. **Engage** (target perceived):
   - Stop hunting. Every **1.0 ± 0.3 s** (Lyra BT wait) choose a strafe point from a grid of ±4 m around the soldier with 1.5 m spacing (Lyra).
   - Keep only points that are reachable, have LOS to the target, and lie within 50 m of it.
   - Pick randomly among the best 25 % by score (closer to the target scores higher). Arrival tolerance 0.25 m.
   - No cover use, no crouch, no grenades, no vehicles.
3. **Fire** (while engaged and the target is in sight):
   - **Reaction delay** 0.5 s from first sight to first shot (choose).
   - Aim at the target's capsule centre (chest), as Lyra AI does. Fire from the soldier's eye.
   - Full-auto rifle: **600 rpm** (0.1 s/round) (choose) in bursts of **3–6** rounds with **0.4–0.8 s** pauses (choose).
   - **Accuracy ramp** (choose): random cone half-angle starts at **6°** at first sight and falls linearly to **1.5°** after **2 s** of continuous sight. Add **+1°** while the soldier moves. Losing sight for more than 1 s resets the ramp.
   - Rifle: the weapon table in spec 04 §5.3 (**12 damage** per hit, magazine **30**, reload **2.1 s** at empty, damage falloff), unlimited spare ammo for soldiers, max damage range **250 m** (Lyra `MaxDamageRange` default). The accuracy ramp above replaces the player spread row for bots.
   - A bullet stops at the first character it hits. Same-team hits do 0 damage.
4. **Dead:** ragdoll (§9). The body is removed after 6 s.

Source: `Plugins/GameFeatures/ShooterCore/Content/Bot/B_AI_Controller_LyraShooter.uasset` (SightRadius 5000, LoseSightRadius 6000, MaxAge 1), `…/BT/BT_Lyra_Shooter_Bot.uasset` (Wait, RandomBest25Pct), `…/EQS/EQS_MoveAgainstEnnemy.uasset` (grid 400 cm, spacing 150 cm, LOS trace filter, distance ≤ 5000 cm), `…/EQS/EQS_FindTarget.uasset`; `Source/LyraGame/Weapons/LyraRangedWeaponInstance.h:175`; `Source/LyraGame/Weapons/LyraGameplayAbility_RangedWeapon.cpp:260-262`.

### 4. Helicopter (3+ stars)

**Spawn and removal** (hunter tick, every 2 s; stars count as 0 after the escape):
- **Spawn** when stars ≥ 3, no helicopter exists, and at least one living human exists.
  - Spawn point: a random living human + (150 m **south**, i.e. −north, 0, +50 m up).
  - Broadcast `Helicopter overhead! Stay out of the spotlight.`
  - Only one at a time.
- **Leave** when stars < 2 (hysteresis: it stays at 2 stars). The helicopter is released straight away, so a new one can spawn while the old one flies off.
  - Leaving = fly along its current forward vector at 30 m/s plus climb 8 m/s, deleted after 12 s.

**Flight** (host, every frame):
- `center` = a ground point. It starts at the spawn point minus 50 m height.
- Each frame:
  - `target` = the living human nearest to `center` (3D).
  - `center` moves toward the target's ground position at ≤ **15 m/s** (`FollowSpeed` 1500).
  - `orbitAngle += 0.22 rad/s` (`OrbitSpeed`).
  - Desired position = `center + (cos·40 m, sin·40 m, 50 m up)` (`OrbitRadius` 4000, `Altitude` 5000).
  - The helicopter moves toward that point at ≤ 30 m/s.
  - Attitude: pitch −8°, roll −12°, yaw = orbit tangent.
- Main rotor turns 1400°/s; the tail rotor turns 1.7× that.
- No collision. Cannot be damaged. Has no weapon.

**Spotlight:**
- Mounted 4.3 m forward and 0.5 m up on the body.
- The aim point moves toward the target's position at a constant **4.5 m/s** (`SpotSweepSpeed`). That is slower than a run (6 m/s) and much slower than a sprint (9.6 m/s), so **running or ducking indoors shakes it off.**
- Look: 400,000 cd, range 120 m, inner cone 4°, outer cone 9°, colour (1, 0.96, 0.85), no shadows.

**Seen rule:** each frame, call `noteSeen()` (lastSeen = now, which holds off decay) if all three hold:
- the aim point is within **6 m 2D** of the target (`SpotRadius` 600), and
- the helicopter is within **64 m 2D** of the target (40 m × 1.6), and
- there is LOS from the light to the target's eye (vehicles block).

Only the current target is tested. The helicopter never adds heat.

Web: a mesh with two spinning rotor nodes, plus a `THREE.SpotLight` (or a fake cone plus a ground decal disc of 6 m radius) aimed at the replicated aim point. Replicate the position, the aim point and `leaving`.

Source: `EscHunters.h:66-72`, `EscHunters.cpp:91-189`, `EscCity.cpp:483-513`.

### 5. Tank (5 stars)

**Spawn and removal** (hunter tick):
- **Spawn** when stars ≥ 5, no tank exists, and at least one living human exists.
  - Pick a random living human ("mark").
  - Candidates: walk points **150–300 m 2D** from the mark.
  - Up to 60 random tries. Accept a point that no human can see (LOS from each human's eye to point + 2 m blocked). After try 40, accept anything.
  - Spawn at the ground point + 1.15 m, facing the mark.
  - Broadcast `A TANK is rolling in. Run.`
- **Removed instantly** when stars < 4.
- Only one tank at a time. It cannot be damaged.

**Body:** box hull 6.6 × 3.4 × 2.2 m (blocks everything). The model origin is on the ground. The turret yaws relative to the hull.

**Per frame** (host):
- `target` = the nearest living human (3D).
- `sees` = LOS from tank + 1.2 m to the target's eye.
- `dist` = 2D distance.
- **Drive** if `!sees || dist > 35 m` (`StopDistance` 3500).
- Turret: turns toward the target at **40°/s**.
- **Fire** when all of these hold:
  - `sees`,
  - `dist < 150 m` (`FireRange`),
  - turret error < 4°,
  - `now > nextShot`.

  `nextShot` = spawn + 4 s for the first shot, then +**6 s** after each (`FireInterval`).

**Driving (greedy street hops):**
- **Re-plan** when there is no waypoint or the tank is within 3 m of the current one:
  - Consider every walk point P with a hop distance of 4–45 m, closer to the target than (current distance − 2 m), and not within 3 m of a remembered blocked point.
  - Sweep the hull (box, height × 0.7, lifted 0.4 m) from here to P against static geometry, dynamic props, vehicles and physics bodies.
  - Among the clear hops, pick the P closest to the target.
  - No candidate → stand still this frame.
- **Move:** hull yaw turns toward the waypoint at **35°/s**. It drives forward at **4.5 m/s** (`Speed`) only while the heading error is < 25°. The move is swept.
- **Stuck:** if it has not moved more than 1 m in 3 s, add the waypoint to the blocked list (keep the last 16) and re-plan.
- **Crush:** any living character hit by the swept move (any team, humans included) is killed outright. It is launched with velocity `tankForward × 18 m/s + up × 9 m/s`, not scaled by armour.

**Shell:**
- Spawns at muzzle = tank + aimDirection × 6.5 m + 1.1 m up, aimed straight at the target's current position (no lead).
- Speed 70 m/s, gravity × 0.1, collision sphere radius 0.2 m, visual sphere 0.5 m diameter. Lives 8 s, then vanishes without exploding.
- Explodes on the first blocking hit other than the tank (ground, buildings, cars, characters).
- **Blast**, for every living character within **8 m 3D** of the blast point (`BlastRadius` 800):
  - `away = normalize(normalize2D(victim − blast) + (0, 0, 0.8))`
  - `falloff = 1 − (dist / 8 m) × 0.5`
  - If `dist < 4 m` (`KillRadius`) **or** `armour < 0.5`: **killed outright** and ragdoll-launched with velocity `away × 32 m/s × falloff × (1 − 0.6·armour)` (`BlastLaunch` 3200).
  - Otherwise (armour ≥ 0.5 and 4–8 m away): **no damage**. The character is shoved with velocity `away × 14 m/s × (1 − armour)`, replacing its current velocity. A plate carrier (exactly 0.5) and heavy soldiers (0.8) survive outside 4 m.
- Visual: explosion FX at 2.5× scale plus a grenade-blast sound on all clients.

Web: for the hull sweep, use an OBB-vs-AABB test against building boxes and car boxes along the hop (or a Rapier `castShape`). Use Rapier kinematic bodies for the hull. Run the shell as a host-simulated projectile and broadcast the blast event (position) to everyone.

### 6. Highway roadblocks (from 3 stars)

The hunter tick runs this after the helicopter and tank.

**Removal pass** (each standing roadblock):
- Remember the last time any living human was within **800 m 2D** of it.
- Remove it if **stars < 2**, or after **30 s** with no human within 800 m.
- Its guards are ordinary soldiers and leave through the normal despawn rules.

**Spawn pass:**
- Only when stars ≥ 3 and fewer than **2** roadblocks stand. At most one new roadblock per tick.
- For each living human **outside every city inset**, try:
  1. **Highway:** the closest point on any highway polyline. It must be within **600 m** of the human, otherwise skip this human.
  2. **Heading:** the velocity of the car they drive, else their own velocity. If that is under 1.5 m/s: the direction away from the nearest city-inset centre. Walk the polyline in the direction whose segment has a positive dot product with the heading.
  3. **Candidates:** every 10 m from **250 m to 450 m** ahead along the polyline. Stop at the polyline's end. Exclude points that are inside any city, within 600 m 2D of another roadblock, or 800 m or more (2D) from the human. Each candidate carries the travel direction at that point.
  4. **Pick:** a random candidate that no human can see (LOS from each eye to point + 1.5 m; characters and cars ignored). If none is hidden, use the **farthest** candidate.
  5. **Place:** origin on the centre line at ground level; local +X = travel direction (the fugitive arrives from −X); local +Y = right.
  6. **Guards:** spawn **3** soldiers through the normal soldier spawn at the guard spots, facing the nearest human. The 24 cap counts dead soldiers not yet removed, so some guards may be refused.
  7. Broadcast `Roadblock ahead on <highway name>!` (names from world.json).

**Geometry** (local metres; road half-width 9 m, i.e. an 18 m highway):
- **Barriers:** 7 jersey barriers, solid, centred at y = −8, −6, −4, −2, 0, 2, 4. Each is 1.9 m wide (2 m pitch with a 0.1 m joint).
  - Box fallback: a base 0.6 deep × 0.35 tall, plus a top 0.25 deep × 0.55 tall sitting on it (top of barrier at 0.9 m).
  - Colour (0.42, 0.41, 0.39), rough.
- **Gap:** an open 4 m lane from y = 5 to 9 m.
- **Boom post:** at (0, 9.3) m, 0.3 × 0.3 × 1.2 m, white.
- **Arm:** 4.3 m long, 0.14 m section, hinged at (0, 9.3, 1.1) m, raised 75° over the gap. Three red (0.6, 0.03, 0.02) stripes 0.16 × 0.6 × 0.16 m at 20 / 50 / 80 % along the arm. The post and arm have **no collision**.
- **Jeeps:** two parked jeep props (solid) at (10, −6) m yaw +15° and (16, −3.5) m yaw −12°.
- **Guard spots:** x = random 3–6 m (beyond the barrier), y ∈ {−5.0, +2.5, +7.0} m each ±0.5 m. The third guard stands behind the gap.

While any roadblock stands, the soldier keep limit is raised by 6 (§2).

### 7. Civilians

#### 7.1 Population
- **Target:** `NumCivilians` = **30** per *occupied city* (city insets that contain at least one living human). **None** for the highway area, because drivers would run them over for heat.
- Wanted = `min(30 × occupiedCities, 60)` (`MaxCivilians`).
- A civilian counts for the area of its nearest human if that human is within **150 m** (`CivilianDespawnDistance`).

**Per director tick** (only when at least one living human exists):
1. **Despawn:** every living civilian that is on foot (not seated in a car) and more than **150 m 2D** from all living humans is deleted, any number per tick.
2. `kept` = living, on-foot civilians within range.
   - If `kept ≥ wanted`: cancel pending followers and stop.
   - If a soldier spawned this tick, stop (one bot spawn per tick).
3. **Group followers first:** while `pendingFollowers > 0` and the group leader is alive and not panicking:
   - `left = (pendingFollowers % 2 == 0)`, evaluated before decrementing.
   - Spawn one follower at the leader's position + leader-right × (left ? −1.2 : +1.2) m, then attach it to the leader with that side.
   - Stop for this tick.
   - If the leader is gone or panicking, `pendingFollowers = 0`.
4. **Ring spawn:**
   - Anchor = a random human of the needy city (`bots < 30`, fewest bots).
   - Skip while `now < nextCivilianSpawn`.
   - Ring: walk points at **25–90 m 2D** (`CivilianSpawnMin` 2500 / `CivilianSpawnMax` 9000), with the same fallback as soldiers.
   - Up to **12** tries for a point hidden from all humans (LOS to point + 1.5 m), else the farthest pick.
   - If the anchor is dead: a random living human, else the home safehouse.
   - Spawn at the ground point + 1 m, random facing.
   - If the new civilian ends up more than 150 m from every human (no street nearby, e.g. on a pier), delete it at once and set `nextCivilianSpawn = now + 5 s`.
   - **Groups:** with probability **0.3** (`CivilianGroupChance`) the new civilian becomes a leader with `pendingFollowers = (rand < 0.33 ? 2 : 1)`. So 30 % of spawns start a pair or, a third of the time, a trio.

**Carjacked drivers:** `spawnCivilianAt(point, flee, fleeFrom)`:
- Allowed while the civilian list (dead ones included) is below 60 + 8 = **68**.
- Spawns at that ground point facing the nearest human and immediately panics away from `fleeFrom` (12 s).

**Corpses:** deleted **20 s** after death. Seated traffic drivers belong to the traffic system and are excluded here.

**Looks:** two **generic** civilian looks (casual "beach" and "elder"), assigned round-robin. No real or identifiable people.

**Display name:** `Civilian`. Unarmed; they never attack.

Web: civilians are lightweight host agents (no physics capsule needed until they die). Snapshot them at ≤ 20 Hz (`CivilianNetUpdateFrequency` 20) and interpolate on clients.

#### 7.2 Civilian behaviour ("think" at 5 Hz, every 0.2 s; turning stays smooth every frame)
Checked in this order:
1. Seated in a car: do nothing (the car moves it).
2. Dead, or `now < frozenUntil` (hands up): stop and stand.
3. **Panicking** (`now < panicUntil`):
   - Speed **5.5 m/s** (`RunSpeed`), jog animation.
   - On arrival, or every **2 s**, pick a new flee goal (see 7.3).
4. Walking gait flag = true while moving, and stays true **1 s** after stopping (so the stop animation is the walk one).
5. **Follower** (has a leader that is alive and within **25 m 2D**, `LeaderLostDistance`):
   - If the leader panics: panic with the leader's threat (12 s).
   - Else follow (7.4).
   - If the leader is dead, gone or more than 25 m away: drop the leader permanently and act solo.
6. **Solo:**
   - Speed **1.7 m/s** (`WalkSpeed`), walk animation.
   - If moving, keep going.
   - If standing before the next decision, look around: every 2–5 s, turn to face a point 5 m away at current yaw ± random 0–100°.
   - At a decision:
     - With probability **0.35** (`IdleChance`) stand idle for **4–12 s** (`IdleMin/MaxSeconds`), first look after 1–3 s. The very first decision is never idle, because they spawn on a road centre line.
     - Otherwise walk: the next decision comes in 1 s. The goal is a random reachable point within **40 m** (`WanderRadius`), arrival tolerance 0.5 m. Fallback: a random walk point 5–40 m away, walked in a straight line.

#### 7.3 Panic and flee
- **`panic(threat, seconds = 12)`** (living civilians only):
  - `panicUntil = max(panicUntil, now + seconds)`, so panic extends and never shortens. Remember `threat`.
  - Switch to run. If not frozen, flee at once.
- **Triggers:**
  - Any gunshot (by a human **or a soldier**) fired within **40 m 2D** of the civilian. Threat = the shooter's position.
  - Being damaged without dying. Threat = the attacker's position, or its own position if unknown, which gives a random direction.
  - Its group leader panicking.
  - A robbery (7.6).
  - Being spawned as a carjacked driver.
- **Flee step:**
  - dir = 2D normalize(self − threat); random if zero.
  - Rotate dir by a random ±30° so crowds don't run in single file.
  - goal = self + dir × **40 m** (`FleeDistance`).
  - Snap the goal to the nearest walkable point within 15 m horizontally / 5 m vertically, else a random reachable point within 40 m, else run straight. Arrival tolerance 1 m.

#### 7.4 Following
- Spot = leader position + leader-right × side + leaderVelocity × 0.5 s. `side` = ±(0.7–1.1 m), random per follower, negative = left.
- Speed = **2.3 m/s** (`CatchUpSpeed`) if more than 2 m from the spot, else 1.7 m/s.
- Move toward the spot (tolerance 0.3 m) while the leader moves faster than 0.3 m/s or is more than 3 m away.
- Otherwise, once arrived, stand and face the leader.

#### 7.5 Health and targeting
- Civilians have **60 HP** (`CivilianHealth`) and no armour.
- They are on team 2, so soldier bullets never hurt them and soldiers never aim at them. Players can kill them, which always costs heat (§1.1).
- Tank shells and tank crushes kill them, with no heat.

#### 7.6 Hold-up (robbery)
- **Prompt:** a human holding a ranged weapon sees `[E] Hold them up` when a living civilian is within **5 m 2D** (`RobRange` 500) and close to the centre of the view: the dot product of the camera forward vector and the direction to the civilian is ≥ 0.9, about ±25.8°.
- **Server check:** the same with 6.5 m reach (5 m + 1.5 m tolerance).
- **On E:** if the civilian was already robbed or is dead, the robber gets the private message `Nothing to take`. Otherwise:
  - Mark it robbed (**once per civilian**).
  - It freezes with hands up for **1.5 s**, facing the robber.
  - Then it panics until frozen-end + **15 s**, fleeing from where the robber stood.
  - The wallet gains random **₪20–300** (integer, inclusive).
  - Heat: +40, or +100 if a soldier sees the robber.
  - The robber gets the private message `They hand over ₪X`.

### 8. Health, armour and damage

- **Base HP:** 100 for fugitives and soldiers (Lyra's default max health), 60 for civilians.
- **Armour** is a value from 0 to 1. It reduces damage by armour/2. The code does this by scaling health: `maxHP = baseHP / (1 − armour/2)`. When armour changes, current HP keeps its fraction of max (MaxHealth is set first, then Health = newMax × fraction).
  - Web: either use the maxHP formula (shown on the HUD), or keep 100 max HP and multiply incoming damage by `(1 − armour/2)`. Pick the maxHP formula to match the original HUD numbers.
- **Player armour:** the shop's **plate carrier** sets armour to **0.5**, so a full-health player goes to **133.3 HP**. The shop lists it as `Plate carrier (wearing one)` (not buyable) while armour ≥ 0.5. It is **lost on death** (the next life starts at 0).
- **Soldier armour:** per variant (§2). Civilians: 0.
- **Knockback scaling:**
  - Weapon deaths: × (1 − armour).
  - Car hits: × (1 − armour).
  - Tank-shell kills: × (1 − 0.6·armour). Tank-shell survivors: × (1 − armour).
  - Tank crush: not scaled.
- No health regeneration (Lyra default, keep it).
- Death = HP reaches 0. Self-destruct kills (tank, car, falling out of the world) deal damage equal to max HP.
- The HUD shows HP / max HP (this replaces Lyra's health widget; layout in the HUD section).

### 9. Ragdoll knockback (the funny part)

On any character death (host):
1. If something already launched the body this life (car, tank shell, tank crush), skip the weapon knockback.
2. `killer` = the last character whose damage lowered this victim's health.
3. **Tier** = the killer's *currently equipped* ranged weapon type:

   | Killer's weapon | Base launch speed |
   |---|---|
   | Shotgun | **26 m/s** (`ShotgunKnockback` 2600) |
   | Rifle | **13 m/s** (1300) |
   | Pistol | **7 m/s** (700) |
   | Anything else (no gun, melee, unknown killer) | **9 m/s** (900) |

4. `M = tier × (1 − victimArmour) × RagdollScale` (1.0).
5. `dir` = 2D normalize(victim − killer). Random if there is no killer or the vector is zero.
6. Launch velocity = `dir × M + up × (0.5 × M)` (`UpKick` 0.5).
7. Broadcast it to every machine. Each one, 0.1 s later, switches the body to ragdoll and adds this **velocity change to every ragdoll body**. Retry every 0.1 s up to 10 tries. After 3 tries, force the ragdoll on if the character is dying.

Other launch velocities:
- **Car hit:** `(carVelocity × 1.2 + up 6 m/s) × (1 − armour)`.
- **Tank shell:** see §5.
- **Tank crush:** `tankForward × 18 + up × 9` m/s.

**No gore:** no blood, decals, dismemberment or screams. Bodies only flop. The ragdoll launch is the comedy.

Web: use a simple Rapier ragdoll (pelvis, torso, head, upper and lower limbs, about 10 bodies with spherical/revolute joints), or a single capsule rigid body with a limp pose. Set each body's linear velocity += the launch vector. Simulate ragdolls locally on every client from the broadcast `{victimId, launchVector}`; they are cosmetic and do not need to sync after the launch. Bodies are removed with the bot (6 s for soldiers, 20 s for civilians) or at respawn for humans.

Source: `EscPawnComponent.cpp:240-331`, `EscPawnComponent.h:97-102`, `EscVehicle.cpp:778-781`, `EscHunters.cpp:330-341,413-429`.

### 11. Death, respawn, team wipe

**When a human goes down:**
- Broadcast `<PlayerName> is down: back at the safehouse in 10 s`.
- **Carried cash bag:** dropped as a pickup at the death spot (on the ground), worth the full bag value. Broadcast `A bag was dropped!`. If the player leaves the game, the bag drops at their last living position.
- **Plate carrier:** lost (armour 0).
- **Delivery package:** lost, with a private message `The package was lost when you went down`.
- **Forged passport:** kept (it is never spent).
- **Weapons:** kept. At death, remember the quick-bar items (pistol/shotgun/rifle, in slot order, no duplicates) and the active slot. Give them back to the next life once it can equip, then select the same slot (slot 0 if that slot is empty). Returned weapons arrive like **newly granted weapons, with a fresh default ammo load.**

**Respawn:**
- Exactly **10 s** (`RespawnSeconds`) after death, the host respawns the player, if they are still dead.
- **Location:**
  - For each *other* living human, find the safehouse nearest to them (2D). Use the safehouse whose distance to its teammate is smallest overall.
  - If no one else is alive (first spawn, solo, team wipe): Tel Aviv's **`Safehouse (The Postel)`**, else the first safehouse.
  - Final point: a random point within **2.5 m** of the safehouse spot, +1 m up, random facing.
- The new life starts at full HP (100, armour 0), unarmed until the weapons come back.

**Team wipe:**
- **Trigger:** a human dies, no *other* human is alive, and the phase is not Escaped. In solo play every death is a team wipe.
- **Effects:**
  - `lost = floor(money / 4)`, `money −= lost`.
  - Heat 0, search reset.
  - Broadcast `Everyone is down! Lost ₪X, the heat is off`.
- The phase is **not** changed: a running finale stays active. The clock simply pauses until someone stands in the radius again, and a boat or crossing finale keeps its 24-soldier army even at heat 0.
- Within 2 s the helicopter leaves, the tank is removed and the roadblocks are removed (stars are now 0).
- Soldiers already in the world stay put while nobody is alive (population updates need a living human). They are culled farthest-first once someone respawns.

### 12. Finales (the three ways out)

**Phases:** `Playing` → `Finale` → `Escaped`. `Escaped` is terminal: the end card stays up for the rest of the session.

**Starting a finale** (only from `Playing`). The price uses `living` = the number of living humans, at least 1, at the moment of purchase. Using a spot requires 2D distance ≤ use radius + 1.5 m on the server.

| Way out (spot) | Use radius | Menu line | Price | Precondition | Hold time | Heat effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Boat** (Jaffa pier tip / Haifa port) | 9 m | `Bribe the captain: ₪X` | ₪15,000 × living | none | **60 s** (`FinaleSeconds`) | forced to 500 (5 stars) |
| **Airport** (Ben Gurion check-in) | 6 m | `Buy tickets: ₪X` | ₪25,000 × living | **0 stars**, and **every living human holds a forged passport** (`passportsMissing == 0`, recounted each tick) | **30 s** (`AirportFinaleSeconds`) | unchanged (stays 0) |
| **Southern crossing** (south edge of Eilat) | 9 m | `Pay the smugglers: ₪X` | ₪10,000 flat for the team | none | **90 s** (`CrossingFinaleSeconds`) | forced to 500 |

**Airport menu priority:**
1. Stars > 0 → info line `Security won't let you in while you're wanted`.
2. Else passports missing → `Everyone needs a passport (forger in Jerusalem)`.
3. Else `Buy tickets: ₪X`.

**Purchase:**
- Pay from the shared wallet. If short: private message `Not enough money (₪X)`.
- Set `phase = Finale`, `escapeSpot` = the spot's position, `finaleKind`, `finalePaid` = the price, and `finaleLeft` = the hold time.
- Boat and crossing: heat → 500 (`Wanted level 5`).
- Then broadcast:
  - Airport: `<Name> bought the tickets (₪X). Boarding in 30 s: stay at the gate and don't get wanted!`
  - Crossing: `<Name> paid the smugglers (₪X). Hold the border for 90 s until the truck comes!`
  - Boat: `<Name> bribed the captain (₪X). Hold the pier until the boat leaves!`

**During the finale** (each director tick, dt = 0.25):
1. **Airport cancel:**
   - If the kind is Airport and stars > 0 at *any* tick: `phase = Playing`, `finaleLeft = 0`, refund `finalePaid` to the wallet.
   - Broadcast `Boarding cancelled: you're wanted! Tickets refunded (₪X). Lose the stars and try again`.
   - Passports stay. Passports are not re-checked during the hold; only stars cancel.
2. **Who holds:** count living humans within **30 m 2D** of `escapeSpot` (`FinaleRadius`).
   - This is **not** "all living players in the zone": **one** living human in the radius is enough to run the clock.
   - Zero → the clock **pauses**; it does not reset.
   - Dead players, or players elsewhere, still escape with the team when the clock ends.
3. `finaleLeft −= 0.25`.
4. **Countdown broadcast:** whenever `ceil(finaleLeft/10)` drops and time is still left, broadcast `<Hold>: <N> s`. N is the new tens value × 10. `<Hold>` is `Boarding` (airport), `Hold the border` (crossing) or `Hold the pier` (boat).
   - Boat: 50, 40, 30, 20, 10.
   - Airport: 20, 10.
   - Crossing: 80 … 10.
5. **Soldiers:** 24 per area for boat or crossing; the airport follows the stars.
   - The helicopter (3+ stars) and the tank (5 stars) come through the normal hunter rules, so the boat and crossing finales get both.
   - Roadblocks appear only for humans on highways.
6. **Menus at escape spots:**
   - The active spot shows `Hold the pier! Ns` / `Boarding... Ns` / `Hold the border! Ns` (N = ceil of time left).
   - Every other escape spot shows `Your ride out is waiting somewhere else`.
7. **HUD objective:** title `HOLD THE PIER` / `BOARDING` / `HOLD THE BORDER` with an m:ss clock, then a hint line:
   - Boat: `Keep someone on the pier until the boat casts off`
   - Airport: `Stay at the gate and keep it quiet: one star cancels boarding`
   - Crossing: `Keep someone at the crossing until the smugglers' truck rolls in`

**Completion** (`finaleLeft` reaches 0 while someone holds):
- `phase = Escaped`, `finalMoney = money`, heat 0, search reset.
- Tier: `finalMoney < 10,000` → **Broke**; `< 60,000` → **Comfortable**; otherwise **Kingpin**.
- Broadcast `ESCAPED <describe> with ₪X: <Tier>`.
- **Describe:**
  - Airport → `on a plane from Ben Gurion`
  - Crossing → `over the southern border`
  - Boat → `by boat from Haifa` if the escape spot lies in the Haifa inset, else `by boat from Jaffa`
- **Afterwards:**
  - All soldiers are deleted every tick. Stars count as 0 for the hunters, so the helicopter leaves, the tank is removed and the roadblocks are removed.
  - `addHeat` is ignored and no soldier can spawn. Civilians keep being managed.
  - Escape menus show `You made it out`.

**End card** (every client while `phase == Escaped`, replacing the normal HUD): the screen is dimmed with black at 60 % opacity. A centred panel at 30 % screen height shows, in order:
1. `ESCAPED` (large, gold)
2. `You got out <describe> with ₪X` (white)
3. The tier name (gold, grey for Broke)
4. `Thanks for playing Escape Israel` (small, grey)

### Addendum: Web navigation for on-foot AI (replaces Unreal's navmesh)

Unreal pathed soldiers and civilians on a Recast navmesh. It was built only around humans (navigation invokers: built
within 220 m of each human pawn and dropped beyond 260 m; `EscPawnComponent.h:103-104`), over every walkable static
surface: ground, sidewalks, roads, piers, hall interiors through their door gaps, and the seabed under the wadeable sea.
"Reachable point", "snap to the nearest walkable point" and "path toward the target" in §3 and §7 all mean that navmesh.
The web has none, so build this instead (host only; clients never path):

**Grid.**
- 1 m cells over the ground plane, in lazily built 64 × 64 m tiles (4 KB each, 1 byte per cell). Cache them, and only
  build tiles within 250 m of a living human, as Unreal did.
- Build in the world worker from the static world description, so the main thread never stalls.
- A cell is **blocked** if its centre lies inside any of these, each footprint inflated by the 0.4 m character radius:
  - building boxes
  - hall walls (with the 3.8 m door gap left open, so interiors are reachable)
  - spot props with collision (01_world.md, Addendum: Spot set pieces)
  - street props with collision (lamps, bins, bollards, kiosks, bus stops, planters, benches, tree trunks)
  - parked roadblock barriers and jeeps (rasterised when the roadblock spawns; cleared when it goes)
  - container stacks, crane legs, mesas, big rocks, edge walls
- Hills and ridges are walkable (slopes are gentle).
- Sea cells (no ground box) are walkable at **4× cost** (Unreal agents could wade). Wander and flee goals are never
  picked in the sea.
- Cars are **not** in the grid: they move. See local avoidance below.

**Queries.**
- `findPath(a, b)`: 8-connected A* with the octile heuristic. Diagonals may not cut a blocked corner. Cap at 20,000
  expanded nodes. If the cap is hit or no path exists, return the partial path to the closest node found.
  - Then string-pull: drop waypoints while a grid line-of-sight (supercover walk over unblocked cells) to the one after
    holds.
  - Agents steer at their own speed to each waypoint with the §3/§7 arrival tolerances.
- `randomReachable(origin, r)`: BFS from the origin's cell over unblocked cells within r (2D), capped at
  (2r + 1)² cells. Pick a uniformly random visited cell. r is 40 m for wander and flee, which is at most about 6,600
  cells.
- `projectToNav(p, 15 m)`: the nearest unblocked cell by a ring search out to 15 m, else none. The 5 m vertical limit in
  §7.3 is moot on a flat grid.
- "Reachable" (soldier strafe points, §3) = the cell is unblocked and `findPath` from the soldier reaches it with a path
  ≤ 1.5 × the straight distance.

**Movement and avoidance.**
- Agents move with the same kinematic character controller as players (capsule 0.4 / 0.9 m), so walls, cars and other
  characters stop them physically.
- Stuck rule (choose): if an agent that wants to move covers < 0.2 m in 1 s, it sidesteps ±90° (random side) for
  0.5 s, then re-paths. Civilians that stay stuck 4 s pick a new wander goal.
- No crowd avoidance beyond that. Unreal used plain path following with no RVO.

**Fallbacks** (as in Unreal when there was no navmesh): a straight-line walk toward the goal (§7.2/§7.3 fallbacks),
and soldiers beyond the built tiles run straight at their target.

**Hunting soldiers** (§3): re-path every 1 s to the target's current position. If the target sits in a car, path to
the car's position. Bots never enter cars.
